### Component Overview: MPU6050 6-Axis IMU
The MPU6050 is an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) that combines:
* **3-Axis Accelerometer:** Measures gravitational force and dynamic acceleration along the X, Y, and Z axes (used to compute roll, pitch, and tilt).
* **3-Axis Gyroscope:** Measures angular velocity (speed of rotation in degrees per second) around the X, Y, and Z axes.
* **Onboard DMP (Digital Motion Processor):** Filters raw sensor measurements internally to provide orientation data.
* **Communication Interface:** Uses the I2C serial bus protocol (SDA data line and SCL clock line), operating natively at 3.3V logic.

---

### Breadboard Architecture
The solderless breadboard connects internal pins using conductive metal clips beneath the plastic casing:
* **Terminal Rows (Columns a–e and f–j):** Each numbered horizontal row (e.g., Row 10) connects five holes together across columns a–e. The center divider separates columns a–e from columns f–j.
* **Power Rails (+ and -):** The continuous vertical columns on the outer edges share connections top-to-bottom. The red strip (+) distributes positive DC voltage (3.3V), and the blue strip (-) distributes common electrical ground (GND).

---

### Hardware Wiring Summary

#### 1. Breadboard Component Placement
* The MPU6050 8-pin breakout header is seated vertically in holes **10e through 17e**.

#### 2. Power and Ground Connections
* **UNO Q 3V3 Pin** ──► **Breadboard Red Rail (+)**
* **UNO Q GND Pin** ──► **Breadboard Blue Rail (-)**
* **VCC (Row 10, pin 10a)** ──► **Breadboard Red Rail (+)** (Supplies 3.3V power to the module)
* **GND (Row 11, pin 11a)** ──► **Breadboard Blue Rail (-)** (Provides common ground)

#### 3. I2C Bus Signals
* **SCL (Row 12, pin 12a)** ──► **UNO Q SCL Pin** (Digital pin D19 / I2C Clock)
* **SDA (Row 13, pin 13a)** ──► **UNO Q SDA Pin** (Digital pin D18 / I2C Data)

#### 4. Address Selection & Auxiliary Lines
* **AD0 (Row 16, pin 16a)** ──► **Breadboard Blue Rail (-)** (Pulls address line low to set standard I2C address `0x68`)
* **XDA (Row 14):** Not connected (Auxiliary I2C data; reserved for external compass)
* **XCL (Row 15):** Not connected (Auxiliary I2C clock; reserved for external compass)
* **INT (Row 17):** Not connected (Interrupt output; data is read via software polling over I2C)
